# Staff

## What is Staff?

- **Staff** is a way to mark your favorite agents for quick access and management.
- Staff is **not** a separate type of agent or a new graph feature. It is simply a way to highlight or bookmark agents you use most often.
- All features and capabilities of agents apply to staff as well.

## Features

- **Direct Access:**
  - You can view and manage your staff directly from the **Staff** menu in the sidebar or navigation.
  - The staff list shows all agents you have marked as staff (favorites).

- **Inbox Integration:**
  - You can interact with staff in the **Inbox** menu, including interrupting or managing events related to your staff agents.

- **Unified Experience:**
  - There is no difference in functionality between a staff agent and a regular agent, except for their visibility and quick access in the staff menu.

## Summary

Staff is a user-centric feature for organizing and quickly accessing your favorite agents. It does not introduce new agent or graph types, but enhances your workflow by making your preferred agents more accessible and manageable.
